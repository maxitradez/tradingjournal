COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

Is PIPBOOK free to use?

Yes. PIPBOOK is currently free to use. You need an account to access your journal.

Does PIPBOOK place or manage trades?

No. PIPBOOK is a journaling and review workspace. You continue to place and manage trades through your broker or trading platform.

Does PIPBOOK guarantee better trading results?

No. It organises the records you enter and helps you review them. Your results depend on your own decisions and market conditions.

Can I keep chart screenshots with my trades?

Yes. You can attach a chart screenshot to a trade entry so the setup and execution have visual context.
